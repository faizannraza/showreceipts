# Responses

This is the dispute lane for the false-done board and for showreceipts itself.

If you are a model vendor, a harness maintainer, a researcher, or anyone who
disputes a board row, the method, or a verdict the tool produced: open an issue
or email the security contact for anything sensitive. Your response gets linked
here verbatim, with your name and framing, not mine. Where a dispute is about a
board row, I re-run the affected rows under the concerns you raise and publish
the delta next to your response.

Two standing commitments:

1. A claim is only ever counted as contradicted on positive contrary evidence
   from the session's own tool log. Absence of evidence is counted separately
   as unverified, never as contradicted. If you find a case where the tool
   marked something contradicted without positive contrary evidence in the log,
   that is a bug, it is a priority, and it will be fixed and disclosed in the
   release notes.

2. Every published number carries its rules version and its date window. When
   the rules change, old rows are re-annotated, never silently recomputed.
